# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who save useful material across many platforms (Reddit, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Substack, GitHub, YouTube, browser bookmarks, personal notes) and lose track of it. Founders, students, researchers, marketers, engineers. They open Cognis in short sessions between focused work: to save something, or to find something they know they saved.

## Product Purpose

Cognis remembers what you learn and brings it back when it becomes useful. It stores saved knowledge with its original source, makes it searchable by words and by meaning, and later surfaces relevant items for the task in front of the user. Success: the user finds a months-old saved item in seconds, and says "that's exactly what I needed."

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, trustworthy. Cognis works beside the user and stays quiet unless it has something worth showing. Copy is plain and specific. It explains why it shows something and never overstates what it knows.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboards with hero metrics, gradient text and glowing cards.
- Notification-heavy productivity apps that compete for attention.
- Cluttered bookmark managers with dense toolbars and tag clouds.
- Chatbot-first interfaces where every action is a prompt.
- Anything that feels like surveillance: hidden capture, vague permissions.

## Design Principles

1. Provenance first. Every item shows where it came from and when it was saved.
2. Explain, don't assert. A result says why it matched, using real evidence.
3. Quiet by default. No badge counts, no fake activity, no invented metrics.
4. One clear next step. Every empty or error state tells the user what to do.
5. The user stays in control. Edit, delete, export and revoke are always one step away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard operation, visible focus, 4.5:1 text contrast in light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, no information carried by color alone.
