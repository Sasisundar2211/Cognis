Connect Cognis

In the Cognis web app, open Account, then Desktop companion, and choose Get a pairing code.

The code works once and expires in 10 minutes. This computer then keeps a revocable token in your system keychain. Remove it any time from Account, Devices.

Ask anything. Cognis answers from what you saved and shows its sources.

Turn on Observe my work and Cognis will bring back saved items when they fit what you are doing. Type /save with text or links to save them. Esc hides this window.
